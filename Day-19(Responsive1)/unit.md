units -

1. px (pixels) - fixed
2. % (percent) - depends upon parent
3. vw (viewport width) - depends upon viewport width's
4. vh (viewport height) - depends upon viewport height's
5. vmax (viewport maximum) - depends upon maximum viewport
6. vmin (viewport minimum) - depends upon minimum viewport
7. em (measure unit (size)) - depends upon the parent size and can be controlled by parent
7. rem (measure unit (size)) - depends upon the root(body) size(1 rem = 16 px) and can be controlled by root
